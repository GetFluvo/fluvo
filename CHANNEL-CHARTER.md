# Channel charter

Fluvo is built by a company that also does migration work. That is a real conflict of
interest for any implementation partner thinking about standardising on it, and it
deserves a straight answer rather than a reassuring silence.

This page is that answer. It is a public commitment, not a marketing line: if we break
it, you can point at this file.

## The problem, stated plainly

If you are an Odoo partner, integrator or accountant, Fluvo is a tool you might put at
the centre of how you deliver migrations. The company behind it also takes migration
work. So the question is fair and obvious: **will the vendor turn up on the other side
of your next pitch, knowing your toolchain?**

## What we commit to

1. **We do not hold the end-client relationship on your projects.** Where you brought
   the project, you are the contracting party and the client's point of contact. If we
   are involved at all, we are behind you.

2. **We do not bid against you on work you brought us into.** If you ask us for help on
   a project — an assessment, a tricky model, assurance on a cutover — we will not
   later approach that client to do the same class of work directly. Being let into
   your project is not lead generation.

3. **Direct end-customer enquiries get referred out.** When an end customer with no
   partner engaged approaches us for an Odoo implementation, we refer them to
   implementation partners rather than taking the implementation ourselves. We are a
   data-migration shop, not an Odoo implementer, and we would rather be the referral
   you remember than the competitor you tolerate.

4. **We do not gate the software.** Fluvo is LGPL-3.0 and anyone may use it — partner,
   accountant, in-house team, competitor, or someone migrating their own company once
   and never again. We will not restrict the tool to a certified channel. Trust is a
   promise we keep, not a door we lock.

5. **Nothing we learn inside your project leaves it.** Your client's data, your mapping
   decisions, your commercial arrangements and your methods stay with you. Anything
   generic we learn may improve Fluvo; nothing specific becomes an example, a case
   study or a sales page without your written agreement.

6. **Paying us never takes anything out of the core.** The engine — everything that
   moves your data — is LGPL-3.0 and is the same engine for everyone. Commercial
   products exist alongside it, for proving and reporting on a migration rather than
   performing one. We will never move an existing core capability behind them.

## What we do take directly

To keep this honest, here is what we do not promise to stay away from:

- **Direct migration work where no partner is engaged**, when a referral is not what
  the customer wants. This funds the open core.
- **Data migration as a subcontractor**, under your name, on your project, when you ask.
- **Sponsored connector development** — you pay for a source system to be supported,
  and the connector ships in the open core where everyone, including your competitors,
  can use it. That is the deal, stated up front.
- **Assessments and assurance work**, for you or alongside you.

If your reading of a situation differs from ours, say so before the engagement rather
than after. We would rather lose a piece of work than a partner.

## Scope and changes

This charter covers the company behind Fluvo and anyone working on its behalf. It does
not bind other users of the software, who owe you nothing under the LGPL.

It is versioned with the repository. Changes arrive by commit, with history anyone can
read — we will not quietly soften it. Where a signed contract says something different
from this page, the contract governs; we will not sign one that contradicts it.

Questions, or a case where you think we have got this wrong: **info@getfluvo.com**.
